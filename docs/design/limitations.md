# Limitations

- Apache-2.0 covers the included software surface only; excluded content remains `DEFER` or source-specific;
- this source snapshot embeds no DOI and does not assert the existence of a Zenodo record;
- global unrestricted Grassmann optimisation is not certified;
- high-precision Riccati evaluation is intended for small audit instances;
- treewidth DP is exact conditional on a valid supplied decomposition;
- the min-fill decomposition is heuristic;
- B&B can be exponential;
- the container candidate was not executed in the recorded local runtime;
- the empirical Generation-2 protocol and campaign remain future phases;
- exact supplied-operand aggregation is not a validated numerical enclosure;
- scenario callbacks, feasibility predicates and economic provenance are not authenticated;
- `supported_points` returns extreme lower-hull representatives, not every tied label;
- explicit probability vectors are not supported by the reference CVaR helper;
- finite E0 software regressions do not establish E1 measurements or E2 monetary value.
