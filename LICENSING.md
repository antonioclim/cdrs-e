# Licensing scope

Apache License 2.0 applies to the software work in this tree: source code under `src/`, tests, scripts, schemas, examples, build and CI configuration, release-engineering files and software-specific documentation under `docs/`, unless an individual file states otherwise.

The software-only surface excludes every path rooted at `paper/`, `evidence/`, `provenance/` or `reports/`. Manuscripts, supplementary documents, article figures, research evidence, private provenance, generated reports, third-party datasets, third-party publications and third-party assets are not conveyed by this repository or its release assets. Their content licence remains `DEFER` or source-specific.

Excluded materials may be retained in private audit wrappers for evidence preservation. Such retention is not public redistribution and not an implicit licence grant. No third-party source dataset is redistributed.

Repository and archival operations are external processes. Their conversational or account-level authorisation is deliberately not encoded as public source metadata.
