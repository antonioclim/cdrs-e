The public software surface excludes every path rooted at `paper/`, `evidence/`, `provenance/` or `reports/`. Manuscripts, supplementary documents, article figures, research evidence, private provenance, generated reports and third-party materials are not conveyed by this repository or its release assets. Their content licence remains `DEFER` or source-specific.

Operational authority for repository creation, upload, tagging, release publication, archival deposition, DOI registration, package-index publication, editorial submission and expenditure is external to this artefact and is not represented by mutable booleans in the public source tree. The artefact records only durable identity, licensing scope and qualification evidence.
